# Evidence before completion

Identify what evidence supports each material claim about correctness, compatibility, or completion. Run fresh checks proportional to the change and inspect their exit status and results; prior runs and confident reasoning are not evidence for the current state.

Inspect delegated work independently, including the resulting diff and relevant behavior. Do not rely only on a subagent's summary.

Report failures plainly. Name checks that could not run, behavior that remains untested, and any claim that is inferred rather than verified.
